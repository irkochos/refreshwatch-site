Refresh the tab. Catch the change.

RefreshWatch reloads any Chrome tab on a timer and tells you the moment the page changes: a new price, a word like “In stock”, an open appointment slot.

Tell it what to watch

Plain auto refresh needs nothing more than an interval. Add a watch and RefreshWatch compares the page after every reload, then alerts you with a notification and a sound.

The whole page

Any change to the visible text. Turn on “ignore changing numbers” so clocks and counters don't set it off.

One element

Point at a price, a seat map or a status line. Only that part counts, so the rest of the page can change freely.

A keyword

Alert when a word appears, like “In stock”, or when one disappears, like “No appointments available”.

Alerts on your phone, and price drops

Add your own ntfy topic, Discord, Telegram or Slack address, or any webhook, and RefreshWatch sends the alert straight there. No account with us, and nothing passes through our servers.

Nothing passes through us

RefreshWatch reads and compares pages on your computer. It has no server of its own. The one thing it ever sends is an alert, to a phone destination you set up yourself.

Read the privacy policy

  • No accountInstall it and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, ads or trackingNo network requests except the phone alerts you set up.
  • Site access one site at a timePlain auto refresh needs no site access. When you turn on watching for a site, Chrome asks you about that one site.
  • Stored locallyRules, settings and the change log stay in Chrome on this computer, and go away when you remove the extension.

Three steps from open tab to alert

  1. Choose an interval

    Click the RefreshWatch icon on the tab. Pick 5 s up to 15 min, a custom time, or a random range.

  2. Choose what to watch

    The whole page, one element you point at, or a keyword. Or nothing at all, for plain auto refresh.

  3. Get the alert

    When the change lands you get a notification and a sound, and refreshing stops. Save it as a rule to have it start by itself next time.
