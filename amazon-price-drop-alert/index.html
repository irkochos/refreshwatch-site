Amazon price drop alert in Chrome

Watching one product on Amazon or another online shop? Keep its page open in a tab. RefreshWatch reloads it, reads the price you picked, and alerts you when it drops below your number.

Updated October 26, 2026

Set up a price alert on a product page

  1. Open the product page and click the RefreshWatch icon. Choose the Price drop preset. It reloads every 15 minutes and watches a Price or number.
  2. Click Pick element and click the price on the page. Pick just the price, not the deal badge, the list price or a monthly payment. The popup shows the number it read next to Now:.
  3. Next to Alert when it, choose drops below and type your price, such as 120. Click Start. Chrome asks to let RefreshWatch read that one site; allow it.

When the price drops below your number you get a desktop notification and a sound. You can also choose rises above, drops at all or rises at all.

Tips for Amazon and other big shops

  • Pick the right price. Product pages show several numbers: the deal price, the list price, the price per unit. If Now: doesn't match the price you care about, pick the element again.
  • Choose the option first. The price can depend on the size, color or seller that's selected. Select the one you want before you pick the price, and check that a reload shows it again.
  • Don't reload too often. Prices rarely change by the minute, and big shops may show a check page to fast reloads. If the picked price isn't on the page three refreshes in a row, RefreshWatch tells you, so a check page or a redesign doesn't fail silently.
  • Watch several products by opening each in its own tab, with its own price and number.

The same steps work on most shop pages that show the price as text. The price drop alert guide covers prices on any site.

Get the price alert on your phone

Switch on Send to my phone and the alert also goes to ntfy, Discord, Telegram, Slack or any webhook, with Chrome left open on your computer. See the phone alerts guide.

Good to know

RefreshWatch checks the page in an open tab while Chrome is running. It doesn't track prices in the cloud, and it keeps no price history chart. The change log on the options page lists the changes it saw, such as $148.00 to $119.00.

It reads the price shown on the page. Coupons, shipping and taxes that appear later in checkout aren't included.

Respect the shop's terms, and leave a few minutes between reloads.

Questions

Is this an Amazon price tracker with price history?

No. RefreshWatch watches the page in your browser and alerts you when the price crosses your number. It keeps a log of the changes it saw, not a price history chart.

Does it work on shops other than Amazon?

Yes. It reads the price from the element you pick, so it works on most product pages that show the price as text.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no RefreshWatch account, and it doesn't need your shop login. It reads the page as Chrome shows it to you.

Does the price alert include coupons and shipping?

No. It reads the price on the product page. Coupons, shipping and taxes added later in checkout aren't part of it.

Will it alert me when my computer is off?

No. Chrome has to be open with the product's tab open. To get alerts while you're away from the screen, turn on phone alerts and leave Chrome running.
