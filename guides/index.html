Guides

Step-by-step guides for RefreshWatch, the free Chrome extension that auto refreshes a tab and tells you when the page changes.

Updated October 26, 2026

Auto refresh

  • How to auto refresh a page in Chrome: set a timer on any tab, from 1 second to 24 hours.
  • A free tab reloader for Chrome: several tabs, each on its own timer, with stop times and saved rules.
  • Auto refresh and click: press up to five buttons, such as “Next month” and “Search”, after every reload.

Alerts

  • Website change alerts: watch the whole page, one element or a keyword.
  • Restock alert: get told when “In stock” or “Add to cart” appears.
  • Price drop alert: watch a price on any page and set your number.
  • Amazon price drop alert: the same price watch on a shop's product page.
  • Appointment slot alert: visa and booking pages, with the cautions that come with them.
  • Phone alerts: send any alert to ntfy, Discord, Telegram, Slack or a webhook.
